## 📌 Overview

ScholarMail is an AI-powered scholarship discovery and admission email generation platform designed for students applying to international universities and scholarships.

The platform helps users:

* Discover scholarships based on their profile
* Estimate realistic eligibility chances
* Generate professional cold emails and scholarship letters
* Explore global scholarship opportunities with AI assistance

Built with a luxury dark-themed responsive UI and integrated with Google's Gemini AI API.
